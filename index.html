The studio

Interior design is as much about the inner landscape of people's minds and hearts as it is about structure and colour.

Baldiwala Edge is the father and son practice of Bakir and Ali Baldiwala. For more than four decades, first as Baldiwala Associates, the studio has designed corporate offices, residences, villas, public spaces and healthcare facilities, making sure nothing ever outweighs the value of human emotion.

Every project starts with the client's brief and ends with a space that fully reflects its purpose, its character and the way it will be used.
